## "AI Office"

"AI Office" has been added to my own dictionary recently. To me, it is a place
where most people use AI for everything, such as brainstorming, coding, writing,
communicating and even thinking. They seem to ask AI about solutions or ideas
for problems even before they actually work on them.

Another point to "AI Office" is that most people do not pay attention to the
amount of energy resources (electricity and water) that a prompt can consume to
produce a response, and they only keep track of the number of tokens, which is
their main concern; if they run out of "virtual, digital, non-existent" tokens
they have burnt, they will be probably "unfunctional and less productive".

## Why I feel lonely?

Work with AI as an assistant, not delegate tasks to AI and review its
solutions/outcomes.

## Consequences of feeling lonely

- Lose fire and motivation.
- Don't want to wake up for work.
- Change job. Cleaner?
